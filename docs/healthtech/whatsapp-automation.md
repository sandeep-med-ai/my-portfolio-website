# WhatsApp Automation

WhatsApp is one of the most practical healthcare communication layers in India. Used responsibly, it can support reminders, education, follow-up, and clinic workflows.

## Useful Workflows

- Appointment confirmations.
- Medicine and diet reminders.
- Follow-up check-ins.
- Report collection instructions.
- Patient education series.

## Design Rule

Automation should feel personal and respectful. It must avoid spam, protect patient privacy, and make it easy to reach a human when needed.
