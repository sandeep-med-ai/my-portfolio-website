# AI Uses in Healthcare

AI becomes valuable in healthcare when it reduces repetition, improves communication, and helps professionals focus on decisions that need human judgment.

## High-Value Areas

- Patient education summaries.
- EMR note structuring.
- Appointment reminders.
- Follow-up message automation.
- Medical knowledge retrieval.
- Clinic operations dashboards.

## Principle

The best AI systems are quiet, useful, and safe. They support the clinician and patient without creating confusion or false authority.
