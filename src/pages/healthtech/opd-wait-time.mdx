# OPD Wait Time

OPD waiting time is not only an operations problem; it affects patient trust, staff stress, and consultation quality.

## Improvement Ideas

- Pre-visit symptom intake.
- Queue status updates.
- Follow-up separation from first consultation.
- Automated reports for routine cases.
- WhatsApp-based reminders and instructions.

## Impact

Even small workflow improvements can make a clinic feel more organized, respectful, and patient-centered.
