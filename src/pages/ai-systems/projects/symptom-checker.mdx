# Symptom Checker

The symptom checker concept focuses on structured intake, not final diagnosis. It helps transform scattered patient complaints into a cleaner clinical summary.

## Intake Flow

- Main complaint and duration.
- Severity and progression.
- Associated symptoms.
- Red flags.
- Existing conditions and medications.
- Suggested next step category.

## Best Use

This is useful before consultation, in telemedicine triage, or in clinic front-desk workflows where better initial information saves time.
