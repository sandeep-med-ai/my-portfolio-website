# Medical RAG

Medical RAG combines curated knowledge sources with AI reasoning. Instead of relying only on a model's memory, the system retrieves relevant references before generating an answer.

## Architecture

- Curated document library.
- Chunking and metadata.
- Vector search.
- Source-grounded response.
- Human review for clinical use.

## Why It Matters

In healthcare, confidence without evidence is dangerous. RAG helps keep answers closer to the source material and makes review easier.
